RED'S TURN
Roll the dice to begin.
How to play: Roll a 6 to bring a token out of base. Tap a highlighted token to move it. Roll a 6 or capture an opponent to earn another turn. Reach the centre with all 4 tokens to win. Pass the phone between players for local multiplayer.
